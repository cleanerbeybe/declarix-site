Compare the cost of two preparation workflows.

Every formula is visible

In-house monthly cost = cases × (preparation/review minutes + average rework minutes) × hourly staff cost ÷ 60 + monthly software/fixed costs + one-off setup ÷ selected months.

Outsourced monthly cost = cases × (provider fee per case + (your oversight minutes + average rework minutes) × hourly staff cost ÷ 60) + provider monthly fee + provider setup ÷ selected months.

Difference = outsourced monthly cost − in-house monthly cost. Positive means the outsourced model costs more under your assumptions. Negative means the in-house model costs more. Per-case cost = monthly total ÷ cases.

Rework is an average across all cases. For example, if you estimate that 10% need 20 minutes, enter 2 minutes. That example is arithmetic, not an observed error rate.
